📈 Employee Analytics & Business Intelligence Using SQL and Python
🔹 Project Summary

This project focuses on extracting meaningful business insights from employee data through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), SQL querying, and data visualization techniques. By combining Python and SQL, the project demonstrates how organizational data can be analyzed to support workforce planning, salary evaluation, and strategic decision-making.

The analysis follows a structured approach that includes data exploration, statistical assessment, business question solving, and visualization of key findings.

🎯 Project Goals

The main objectives of this project were:

Explore employee-related data to identify trends and patterns.
Perform descriptive statistical analysis.
Use SQL queries to answer practical business questions.
Visualize workforce and salary-related metrics.
Generate actionable insights for business decision-making.
📊 Dataset Information

These fields provide the foundation for analyzing workforce distribution, compensation trends, and departmental performance.

💻 SQL-Based Business Analysis

A series of SQL queries were written to address common business questions related to employee management and compensation.

Business Problems Addressed
Highest Paid Employee

Identified employees receiving the highest compensation within the organization.

Lowest Paid Employee

Determined the minimum salary level among employees.

Average Salary Analysis

Calculated the overall average salary across the workforce.

Department Workforce Distribution

Measured the number of employees working in each department.

Department Salary Expenditure

Evaluated total salary spending across departments.

High-Earning Employees

Identified employees whose salaries exceeded a specified threshold.

Salary Ranking

Ranked employees according to their compensation levels.

📌 Key Findings

The analysis revealed several important insights:

The IT department accounted for the highest overall salary expenditure.
HR and IT departments contained the largest number of employees.
The average employee salary was approximately ₹92,300.
Multiple employees earned salaries exceeding ₹100,000.
Salary distribution varied significantly across departments.

These findings provide valuable information for compensation planning and workforce management.
